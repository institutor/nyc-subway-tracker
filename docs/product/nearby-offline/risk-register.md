## Purpose and authority

This register records the rider consequence, approved product response, accountable owner, required evidence, and release effect for risks that affect the nearby and offline Release 1 package. It does not replace the narrower [Arrival Truth risk register](../quality/arrival-truth-risk-register.md), create accessibility or guidance truth, grant map or brand rights, authorize personal-data use, or convert an exclusion into a tested result.

The [approved product specification](../../superpowers/specs/2026-07-30-nyc-subway-train-time-tracker-design.md) controls every response below. Risk review follows the [product artifact review and approval policy](../review-and-approval-policy.md). The binary release effect is recorded in [Release 1 readiness](release-1-readiness.md).

## Risk action rule

- **Likelihood** reports only what current evidence supports. `Unknown — not measured` is not Low.
- A product response is a required contract, not proof that the control works.
- A risk is not controlled until one fixed working-product and dependency package supplies the named evidence and every required reviewer accepts it.
- Missing, inconclusive, not-observed, not-measured, or not-run critical evidence blocks release.
- Excluded capabilities remain absent. Their omission is not a measured safety result, and appearance in the candidate is a release-contract failure.

## Stop-rule alignment

The [Release 1 stop blockers](release-1-readiness.md#stop-blockers) take precedence over risk scoring. In particular:

- one false bypass, unlabeled fallback, hidden passenger-serving direction, known-outage accessible recommendation, or offline-live implication stops release;
- missing critical acceptance evidence stops release even when no failure has yet been observed; and
- unresolved map or brand rights stop public use and Release 1.

No risk in this register has a waiver, accepted residual-risk decision, or closed evidence state.

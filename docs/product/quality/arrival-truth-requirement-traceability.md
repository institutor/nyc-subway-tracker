## Purpose and authority

This artifact traces every arrival-truth requirement to one decision owner, one controlling artifact, at least one direct case, and the evidence field that must be recorded. It also proves that every truth-owned Section 31 scenario has a product contract, a narrower decision owner, and a Pending evidence record.

The [approved product specification](../../superpowers/specs/2026-07-30-nyc-subway-train-time-tracker-design.md) controls every conflict. A case or trace row never overrides its owner artifact. All rows remain **Draft** or **Pending** until the fixed-version evidence and mandatory reviewer decisions required by the [review and approval policy](../review-and-approval-policy.md) exist.

## Integrated release assertions

Traceability review fails if any of these assertions is false:

1. No static or supplemented trip creates or restores a live row inside a healthy replacement period.
2. No Live, Expected, Holding, Uncertain, Scheduled, or guidance row survives a resolved or materially unresolved stop-service veto.
3. Admission finishes before ordering; only admitted Live and Expected rows consume next-three slots.
4. Every missing, frozen, unavailable, suppressed, and fallback state has the evidence-supported rider explanation in the catalog.
5. Every feed, movement, Due, disappearance, bulk-drop, alert-currency, and schedule-currency threshold has an exact-boundary and immediate just-over case.
6. Every count gate has a lower-count control and the required-count outcome.
7. Small skew, materially overdue, repeated failure, unusual-dwell margin, dwell percentile, and sustained-outage count or duration remain uncalibrated; only the approved last-coherent-snapshot age and cause-based precedence control fallback transition.
8. Every executed result retains the provenance, actual visible outcome, prohibited checks, recovery evidence, failure, correction, and rerun required by ATR-R36 through ATR-R39.

The catalog and this trace remain Pending until all assertions are supported by durable observed evidence for the same fixed reviewed version.

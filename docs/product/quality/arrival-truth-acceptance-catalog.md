## Purpose and authority

This catalog is the integrated acceptance inventory for the arrival-truth workstream. It consolidates approved-specification scenarios 1–20, 40–44, 49, and 50 without changing their expected outcomes. It also records scenarios 45–48 and 51 as Pending dependencies owned by other workstreams.

The [approved product specification](../../superpowers/specs/2026-07-30-nyc-subway-train-time-tracker-design.md) controls every conflict. The [core arrival contract](../arrival-truth/core-arrival-contract.md) and the narrower owner named in each mapping control product behavior. This catalog records expected decisions; it does not create a new rule, approve a scenario, or convert expected prose into observed evidence.

Every row remains **Pending** until a fixed reviewed product version is exercised, the actual visible result and every prohibited-outcome check are recorded, failed runs retain their correction and rerun links, and all required reviewers decide on the same evidence set.

## Uncalibrated thresholds preserved

The following phrases remain intentionally nonnumeric. This catalog does not assign a value:

- **small skew allowance** for authoritative live-source time;
- **not materially overdue** for an origin-terminal Expected train;
- **repeated update failures** as a named feed invalidator;
- unusual dwell as the greater of **two minutes plus a small operating margin** or the **observed high-percentile dwell** for the station, route, direction, and operating period; and
- **sustained outage** has no independent count or duration. Only the approved whole-second age of the last complete coherent snapshot and the cause-based preservation-versus-fallback precedence control the transition.

Any numeric proposal requires observed evidence and fresh Product and Data Quality review of the owning Draft policy and cases.
